view of Liberty Jaspers has in recent years — with great courage against the trend of public opinion — placed the national aim of re-uniting divided Germany below the far weightier concern for the freedom of man. The national State as such is unimportant. What counts is a true tradition of liberty for the individual human being. In Jaspers' work nobody will ever find a programme of political strategy or indeed any kind of political programme. Anything that might appear to be of this nature will only be a means of making clear what ought to be the basic meaning and fundamental norm of any policy, any strategy — namely the absolute value of the possibilities of liberty. Some people in Germany have believed that with these opinions Jaspers had disowned his Fatherland. But the contrary is the case; there could be no greater loyalty than his towards a certain kind of Germany — of which he seemed to find some trace in the small town of Basel in Switzerland — that is, a diversified Germany with small, limited political units, a Germany that knew how to value her diversity, her local and ordinary attachments, as the most fruitful soil for human freedom and its possibilities. Always the courageous figther against the stream, always despising the lying sentimentality of the knights of "eternal peace", Jaspers refused to appeal for unilateral abolition of atomic weapons, for peace at any price. On the contrary — he did not, as his opponents claimed, speak in favour of the bomb, but he championed a true peace, one that can only be of a supra-national nature. And he spoke for the Freedom that refuses to yield itself up here and now for fear of a future apocalypse, and which today as ever before claims the right to make its own decisions according to the situation at any particular moment.

And that is not the only way in which Jaspers has applied his philosophy to the actual happenings of daily life. He has lived among the great philosophers of the past, understanding them, interpreting them, making them his own — as with his existential friends — lived with them in reverence and in discussion, and has generously allowed us all to share them with him. He has been a lifelong friend of those still alive. And finally his loyalties reach their peak in his marriage. Today, as ever, his wife is by his side, devoted to him and yet, as ever, totally herself. Wer are experiencing a great, a beautiful and a happy day.
