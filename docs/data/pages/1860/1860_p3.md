FORATOM VIII

# NUCLEAR ENERGY-
## EUROPE AND THE WORLD

8th Foratom Congress
June 20-24, 1982
Palais de Beaulieu, Lausanne, Switzerland

---

### List of Papers presented:

#### OPENING CEREMONY

Opening address by Federal Councillor Leon SCHLUMPF, Minister of Energy, Switzerland.

#### OPENING SESSION

Energy Challenge and International Economic Evolution in the Eighties by speaker Raymond BARRE, ancien Premier Ministre du Gouvernement Français, Prof. d'Université, Paris (France)

The Environmental Aspects of Nuclear Energy within the Framework of the Environmental Problems of Other Energy Sources by speaker John DUNSTER, CB, Deputy Director General, Health and Safety Executive, London (GB)

Public Opinion and Nuclear Energy: Psychological, Ideological, Social and Politic Problems by speaker Jeanne HERSCH, professeur honoraire de philosophie à l'Université de Genève (Switzerland)

Energy and the Third World by speaker Dr. Hoachim GRAWE, Director General, Head of Department, Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation Bonn

#### 2nd SESSION

Light Water Nuclear Power Reactors Throughout the World: State of the Art and Experience by speaker Jean-Claude LENY, Directeur Général de Framatome, Paris (France) (a paper prepared jointly with Remy CARLE, Directeur de l'Equipement d'Electricité de France, Paris)

Heavy-water Reactors by speaker Ulrich FISCHER, Senior Project Leader Heavy-water Reactors, Kraftwerk Union AG, Erlangen (FRG)

Gas Cooled Reactors - a Review by speaker Dr. Norman L. FRANKLIN, Managing Director, National Nuclear Corporation Ltd., Knutsford, Cheshire (GB)

Fast Breeder Reactors, the Energy Goal for the '90s by speaker Dr. Ing. Bruno MUSSO, Secretary General, Ansaldo Group, Genoa (Italy)
