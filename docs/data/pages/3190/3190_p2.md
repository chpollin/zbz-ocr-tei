Jeanne Hersch

Jaspers, University Youth and the Human Condition

Jeanne Hersch is Professor of Philosophy at the University of Geneva and a former Visiting Professor at Hunter College, New York, and Pennsylvania State University. Between 1966 and 1968 Professor Hersch was Head of the Division of Philosophy of UNESCO and between 1970 and 1972 Swiss Representative on the Council of UNESCO. She has been politically active in the European Federalist Movement. Her many publications include *L'Illusion philosophique*; *Une philosophie de l'existence*: Karl Jaspers; *L'Être et la forme*; *Idéologies et réalité*. She has translated several works of Karl Jaspers and of Czeslaw Milosz.
